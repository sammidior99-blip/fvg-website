About us

Academic excellence, empowering the future.

Rooted in Qingdao, China, Firmament Voyage Global is a premium consultancy that brings together the world's leading academic resources to plan international education for ambitious families. We set aside the production-line approach of traditional agencies. Guided by our core belief that academic excellence empowers the future, we have built an advisory network spanning higher education in East and West.

Our core strength

A faculty network from the Ivy League, Oxford and Cambridge

Our fundamental advantage is the joint mentoring system we have built with leading professors from Harvard, Yale, Princeton and Oxford (HYPO). Our core advisory panel brings together tenured professors, former admissions committee members and leading scholars from these four universities. Beyond shaping the frontiers of global scholarship, they take an active part in each student's long-term academic planning and research profile. They assess every candidate's potential through a world-class lens, so that students stand out in a fiercely competitive field with academic strengths that are genuinely rare.

Our services

End-to-end support, from academic insight to admission

Drawing on our professors' own academic networks, we go beyond standard application support, with research-group-level profile building and essays reshaped from an admissions officer's point of view:

Research pathway

Placement in remote or on-site research projects with HYPO, Oxford and Cambridge laboratories, leading to strong papers and letters of recommendation.

Tailored progression planning

No template school lists. Overseas professors and senior advisors work together to map a global pathway from high school through master's and doctoral study.

Cross-cultural academic readiness

We build the critical thinking and academic writing skills that top Western universities expect, early, so that a successful application leads straight into success in study.

Services

Tailored educational solutions for every stage of success.

From individual consultations to comprehensive, long-term educational planning, our services are designed around each student's unique needs and aspirations. Whether seeking focused support or a fully integrated strategy, every journey begins with a personalised consultation.

01

International school & curriculum planning

Choosing a school and curriculum shapes everything that follows. We help families compare options and choose with confidence.

What's included

  • Comparing curricula such as IB, A-Level, AP and IGCSE
  • School shortlists matched to the student's needs
  • Admissions timelines, tests and interview preparation
  • Advice on moving between schools or systems
02

Academic & GPA support

A strong, steady academic record opens doors. We help students make smart subject choices and stay on track.

What's included

  • Subject and course selection guidance
  • Study planning and time-management habits
  • Identifying areas for improvement early
  • Preparation strategies for key assessments
03

Enrichment planning

Meaningful activities show who a student is beyond grades. We help students choose experiences that reflect real interests.

What's included

  • Exploring interests and possible academic directions
  • Competitions, research and independent projects
  • Summer programmes and volunteering
  • Building a coherent profile over time
04

Undergraduate & postgraduate application guidance

We guide students through each part of the application process, so nothing important is missed and every piece shows their best.

What's included

  • University and programme shortlists
  • Personal statements, essays and CVs
  • Interview preparation
  • Deadline and document management
  • Comparing offers and making a final choice
  • Graduate programme selection and research background
  • Research statement (SOP), personal statement and CV
  • Scholarship applications
05

University transition support

Getting in is only the beginning. We help students prepare for the academic and personal changes of university life.

What's included

  • Pre-departure planning and checklists
  • Independent study and academic skills
  • Settling into a new country and campus
  • Check-ins during the first term
06

Research & professor-led academic mentoring

Students interested in research need more than a topic. We match each student with a mentor in their area of interest and guide them from choosing a question to presenting the results.

Suitable for: high school and undergraduate students interested in academic research

What's included

  • Mentor matched to interests and intended field
  • Choosing a topic and research question
  • Literature reading and research methods
  • Organising and presenting the results
07

Summer academic programmes

Use the summer to work with university-level material and, with a mentor's guidance, complete a concrete piece of learning or research.

Suitable for: secondary students who want to strengthen their academic profile over the summer

What's included

  • Summer research projects
  • Professor-led teaching and small-group mentoring
  • Progress feedback along the way
  • Final presentation of results
08

US high school curriculum & credit planning

Students studying in China can still prepare for university abroad. We help plan online US high school courses and credits so that more options stay open.

Suitable for: secondary students in China planning to study abroad

What's included

  • Choosing an online US high school programme
  • Course and credit management
  • GPA and AP planning
  • A timeline that connects to overseas university applications
09

Boarding school applications & transition

From choosing a school to settling in after arrival, we support students and families at every step.

Suitable for: students planning to attend boarding high school abroad

What's included

  • Boarding school shortlists and applications
  • Visa guidance
  • Accommodation and guardianship arrangements
  • Pre-departure preparation and support settling in

Our commitment

We do not guarantee any particular admission result. What we commit to is planning, mentoring, progress management and academic training that we can actually deliver, with a clear explanation of what the student needs to complete at each stage.
